LEXFIT · Onboarding · Layout & sizing spec

The flow is right. The geometry is not.

The implemented onboarding uses the correct copy, the correct question order and the correct split-screen idea — but the shell has no fixed geometry, so every step lays itself out differently. The body column and the action bar sit on two different axes, content is top-aligned inside a full-height pane that leaves ~540px of dead space, and the type scale is roughly a third larger than drawn. This document is the missing half of LEXFIT Onboarding Wireframe: not what the screens say, but exactly how big everything is and where it sits — on desktop and on mobile.

One axis, not two

Defect 01 is the one that reads as "broken" at a glance. A multi-step flow is judged on whether things stay still between steps — two axes guarantee they do not.

Cap the column, not the pane

The pane must fill the viewport for the split to work. The content column must not. That single distinction removes defects 02 and 03.

The screenshots are a tall viewport

1440px tall exaggerates the voids — but they exist at 900px too, just smaller. Do not treat this as a large-monitor-only bug.

Copy and order are correct

Nothing in this document changes a question, an option or the sequence. The wireframe's flow survives unchanged.

The pane fills, the column doesn't

Two different jobs. The pane owns the background and the 50% split; the column owns the reading measure and the rhythm. Conflating them is the whole bug.

480px is a reading measure

At 17px body copy that is ~62 characters — the standard comfortable line. The 600px rows in the build push descriptions to three lines and make the eye travel.

The header row is 40px on every step

Including the steps with no progress bar (8, 9, 10), where it holds only the back arrow. The question title must land on the same y every time.

Left panel below 1280

Hero drops 48→40px and the lede to 16px. Do not let the stat row wrap — if it would, drop to two stats before you let it break.

Three step types, one skeleton

Option list, reveal, plan picker. The shaded bands are at identical y in all three. That is the whole test — if a band moves, the geometry is wrong.

Body scrolls, chrome does not

Step 10b and the auth form exceed 760px. They scroll inside the body row while the header and action bar stay put.

The action bar may grow

Adding a meta line or a ghost button grows it downward from its border-top. The body row absorbs the difference, so the title still does not move.

Step 9 is the existing Auth pane

Its form is used as built — but it must adopt this column, not bring its own. Same 480px axis, same header row, same action bar.

Drop the brand panel, don't stack it

The auth handoff already warned that stacking pushes the form below the fold. Under 1024 the panel is not shown at all — the mark in the header carries the brand.

100dvh, not 100vh

dvh accounts for the collapsing mobile URL bar. With vh the sticky action bar sits under the browser chrome on first paint.

The action bar is opaque

It overlaps scrolling content. A translucent bar over option rows reads as a rendering fault — solid white, 1px border-top, no blur.

Tablet is not a small desktop

768–1023 keeps the mobile stack and only adds a 440px measure. Two panes at 900px would give the form a 450px pane — narrower than the mobile screen it replaced.

Two type sizes per role, no more

One desktop value, one mobile value, tablet interpolated only for the h1. Fluid clamp() on question text is what produced the 40px h1 in the build.

44px is a floor, not a target

Every row and button here clears it. The audience is 35+ and often mid-movement — 64–68px rows are the reason the flow feels easy.

Tick shape carries the semantics

Circle for single-choice, rounded square for multi-select. Step 5 is the only multi-select — it must not look like the others.

The split needs 1024

At 1024 each pane is 512 — just enough for the 480 axis minus padding. Below that the form gets narrower than a phone, which is the failure the auth README predicted.

Nothing grows past 1600

The axis is capped, the column is capped. A 4K monitor shows more background, not bigger text — which is what the build's vw sizing got wrong.

W7

Layout rules

L-RULE 01

One axis per pane.

Header, body and action bar are children of the same measured column. No descendant sets its own width or margin-inline.

FixesDefect 01 — the 274px offset between body and CTA.
L-RULE 02

The pane fills the viewport; the column does not.

Panes are 100dvh. The content column is capped at 760px and centred with margin-block:auto.

FixesDefect 02 — the 540px void above the CTA.
L-RULE 03

The title lands on the same y on every step.

Header row is 40px whether or not it holds a progress bar; body padding-top is always 32.

EvidenceStability between steps is what makes a sequence feel like one screen changing rather than ten screens loading.
L-RULE 04

Only the body scrolls.

The shell has overflow:hidden. Header and action bar are never scrolled off.

FixesDefect 08 — the page-level scroll.
L-RULE 05

Fixed type scale. No viewport units on text.

Two values per role — desktop and mobile — with the h1 interpolated once for tablet.

FixesDefect 06 — the 64px brand h1 and 40px question h1.
L-RULE 06

Below 1024 the brand panel is dropped, not stacked.

The mark moves into the 56px header. Nothing else from the panel survives.

EvidenceThe auth handoff's own warning about stacking pushing the form below the fold.
L-RULE 07

The action bar owns the bottom inset.

padding-bottom:calc(12px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)), opaque background, 1px border-top.

EvidenceHome-indicator overlap is the most common iOS onboarding defect.

What not to do

✕Centring the CTA in the pane while the body is left-aligned. The single most visible defect in the current build.
✕Letting the content column stretch to 100dvh. It produces the dead space; cap it at 760 and centre it.
✕clamp() or vw on question type. Two fixed values are correct; fluid text made the h1 40px.
✕Stacking the brand panel above the form under 1024px. Drop it.
✕A translucent or blurred mobile action bar. It sits over scrolling rows — keep it solid.
✕Option rows wider than 480px. Descriptions wrap to three lines and the eye has to travel.
✕100vh on mobile. Use 100dvh or the action bar hides under the URL bar.
✕A second loud button in the action bar. One primary; „Most kihagyom" is a text link.
Two things this spec does not decide

Whether the desktop action bar should instead sit immediately below the content (rather than pinned to the column's bottom edge) on the three short steps — pinned is specified here for step-to-step stability, but it leaves a visible gap on step 4. And whether the left panel's hero copy changes per step or stays fixed; fixed is drawn, because a changing panel breaks the "one screen" illusion the split exists to create.
