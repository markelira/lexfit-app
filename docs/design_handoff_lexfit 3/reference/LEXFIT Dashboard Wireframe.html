LEXFIT · Dashboard redesign · Step 1 of the build — structure only

The familiar skeleton.

Low-fidelity layout for the new LEXFIT dashboard. Every region below is placed because of a specific finding in the familiarity research — nothing here is a taste decision. No colour, no photography, no copy polish: this pass fixes where things live and what shape they are. Visual design comes after this is signed off.

Step 01

Global shell — the chrome that never moves

Four things must be on screen at every moment, on every route: where I am, where else I can go, how I search, and who I am. The current build hides two of them. This is the frame everything else is drawn inside.

F-01 · RULE 01

No collapse control. The rail is permanent. NN/g measured hidden desktop nav at 27% usage vs 48% visible, ≥39% slower tasks, +21% perceived difficulty.

F-02 · RULE 03

Search moves into the shell. Open text field, top, toward the right — Nielsen's canonical Jakob's Law pattern. It was previously reachable only inside Videótár.

F-03 · RULE 02

Kezdőlap, not Foundation. Four destinations in the user's words. Foundation is a programme living inside Kezdőlap — a product, not a place.

F-04

Avatar top-right. Circular, opens Profil / Beállítások / Segítség / Kijelentkezés. The most engraved position on the web.

F-08

Streak in the header. Flame + day count, visible on every route. One daily behaviour, reinforced everywhere.

KEEP · F-10

Icon plus a permanently visible label on every nav item. Never hover-only, never icon-only — not at any breakpoint.

Step 02

Kezdőlap — billboard + stacked rows

The chassis stays: a billboard hero over vertically stacked horizontal rows. This is the layout the audience meets nightly on Netflix, and the literature calls it the de-facto standard for streaming recommendation. What changes is the order of the rows and the content of the cards.

KEEP · F-09

Billboard + stacked rows survives untouched. It is the correct chassis and the audience already knows how to read it. Only the row internals change.

RULE 05

One loud action per screen. "Edzés indítása" is the only solid-fill button in the viewport. Nielsen: a quiet primary CTA gets overlooked because every other app shouts.

RULE 07

Ring + week strip, high on the page. The "am I on track?" mental model is already installed by Apple Fitness and banking apps — reuse it rather than invent.

Netflix · resume

"Folytatod" is the first row under the hero, with a progress bar along the bottom edge of each card. The most-used row on Netflix maps exactly to mid-programme users.

F-08 save pattern

A "+" beside every card's text block sends it to Listám. One click, no dialog, visible state change (+ → ✓).

Card = Variant B

All rows use the selected YouTube-geometry card — four per row rather than five, since B is taller. Title only under the thumbnail; the second line appears only when there is a state to report.

Row order rationale

Resume → today's programme → my saved → low-commitment → community. Each row answers a likelier question than the one below it.

Step 03

Card & row anatomy — Variant B, selected

This is the highest-leverage change in the whole document. The SIGIR '26 eye-tracking analysis found the top-left item of a row was examined by 90.82% of users while its own heading was examined by 25.77% — at least 65% of people looked at the artwork and never at the label. Variant B (the YouTube video-item) is the selected direction: nothing depends on hover, everything is permanently legible, and the geometry is the most-parsed card layout on the internet. Differentiation comes from the content of the slots, never their position — see the separate Variant B differentiation document.

Step 04

Mobile — a bottom tab bar, not a squeezed sidebar

Below ~840px the rail becomes a fixed bottom tab bar: four destinations, icon plus permanent label, filled icon for the active tab, 44×44px minimum targets. This is what every Hungarian phone user touches daily in Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and WhatsApp. Search and avatar move into a slim top bar.

RULE 06 · F-07

Fixed bottom tabs, 3–5 destinations. Apple HIG has long recommended the tab bar; Material moved to bottom nav for primary destinations in 2016.

F-10 at every breakpoint

Labels stay under the icons. The temptation to drop them on small screens is exactly where the familiarity is lost.

F-02 on mobile

Search stays reachable in one tap from the top bar of every screen — it does not retreat into Videótár again on small screens.

Targets

44×44px minimum on every tab, the save "+" and the play control. Carried over from the audit already run on the Apple TV page.

Step 06

What to build, in what order

The first three steps are removals and moves, not a redesign — they can ship before anything is visually designed, and they are the ones that decide whether a first-time user feels lost.

Ship 1

Un-hide the shell

Delete the collapse toggle. Move search into the header. Move the avatar top-right. Rename Foundation → Kezdőlap.

Ship 2

Rebuild the card

Variant B everywhere: "28 PERC" badge, Alexa avatar, title only, save "+", progress bar, MAI ribbon. Second line for state only.

Ship 3

Re-order the rows

Folytatod first, then the Foundation week, then Listám, then short workouts, then community.

Ship 4

Mobile tab bar

Bottom tabs below 840px, labels kept, 44px targets, slim top bar for search + avatar.

Ship 5

Streak & forgiveness

Flame in the header; the rest day protects the streak instead of breaking it.

Open question before visual design

One structural decision is not settled by the research and should be tested with five real users: whether the week strip belongs above or below the first row. Cheap to test, expensive to guess. (The fourth destination is now Kihívások — the Szavazz Magadra archive; see its own skeleton.)
